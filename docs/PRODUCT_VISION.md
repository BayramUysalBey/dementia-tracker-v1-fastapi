# Dementia Tracker: Product Vision & Target Audience

**"Dementia Tracker" is not just a record-keeping tool, it's a Care Sustainability System.** By bridging the gap between clinical data and emotional well-being, the application delivers value through four key pillars.

---

## 1. Target Audience
* **Primary target user:** Family caregivers.
* **Secondary target user:** Early-stage patients.

## 2. Tracked Data Points

### Patient Data Points
* Medication schedules.
* Daily mood and confusion levels.
* Physical activity, daily tasks, and sleep patterns.

### Caregiver Criteria and Habits
* Caregiver stress levels/burnout indicators
* "How are you?" Features (daily note-taking)
* Daily regular exercise (gym or home)
* Meditation and breathing exercises
* Chill mode (free time)
* End-of-day impressions (reporting)
* Reporting on the 30th of each month and emailing to the caregiver in `.pdf` format (and deleting the data to save space in the database)

---

## Product Value Proposition

### 1. Safety and Anxiety Reduction (For the Caregiver)
Real-time GPS and Geo-Restriction integration ensures the core value of "Continuous Peace of Mind.”
* **Impact:** By receiving instant alerts when a patient leaves a defined "Safe Zone," the caregiver is relieved of the high stress load of constant visual surveillance, directly reducing the cognitive load and daily anxiety associated with the patient's "wandering."

### 2. Clinical Accuracy and Care Continuity (For the Doctor)
The monthly PDF reporting feature transforms anecdotal observations into "Evidence-Based Data."
* **Impact:** Caregivers often may not remember specific details during short doctor appointments. By providing a structured PDF containing medication adherence, sleep patterns, and mood swings for 30 days, the application enables physicians to make more accurate adjustments to treatment plans, thus providing higher quality care for the patient.

### 3. Burnout Prevention and Sustainability (For the Family)
Through "How are you?" features and "Relaxation Mode" and Meditation tracking, the application treats the caregiver as a critical component of the medical system.
* **Impact:** A healthy caregiver is the patient's best resource. By monitoring the caregiver's stress levels and encouraging regular exercise and leisure time, the application acts as an "Early Warning System" for burnout. This ensures that care remains sustainable in the long term and prevents the collapse of the home care environment.

### 4. Operational Efficiency (Backend Value)
Thanks to the automatic data cleanup feature after reporting, the system provides a high-performance and low-cost infrastructure.
* **Impact:** For the user, this means a fast and responsive application that doesn't slow down over time. For the provider, this means a lean database architecture that prioritizes current and relevant data and archives historical data in portable PDF formats.
